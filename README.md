# lab

How Step Zero Space decides which ideas get built, and when they get killed. One folder per idea, written in the open.

## How an idea gets in
1. It is captured in the private idea bank.
2. It runs once through the Chief of Staff: research, a score out of 20, and a red-team pass.
3. If it scores 15 or more and Arjun says yes, it gets a folder here with three files:

| File | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| `THESIS.md` | What problem, for whom, why now, and the one-line bet |
| `KILL.md` | What result would end it, and the date we decide |
| `PILOT.md` | Who tests it, what they try, what happened |

## How an idea gets killed
On its decision date, the numbers in `PILOT.md` are checked against `KILL.md`. Any kill criterion met → the idea stops and gets a short post-mortem in its folder. No extensions without a written reason.

## Rules
- **One active idea at a time** (`ACTIVE.md`).
- No plans, prices, private data or employer material in this repo.
- Templates are in `templates/`.
